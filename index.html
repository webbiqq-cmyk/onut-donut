Our story

A Riffa kitchen, obsessed with the fry.

Onut started as a two-person kitchen testing dough hydration at midnight. Today we still proof everything by hand, fry in small batches, and glaze to order so nothing sits — because a donut's best ten minutes are its first ten.

Every flavor on the board started as a scribbled idea on the prep-room whiteboard — from the original Ring to the seasonal Cloud drops our regulars wait on.
